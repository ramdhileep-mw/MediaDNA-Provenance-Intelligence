Quick Guide

Media monitoringTracking what people, publishers and organisations publish about a topic, brand or event.
MentionOne monitored item, such as an article, post, press release or web page.
SourceThe publisher, account or website that published a mention. Several mentions may come from the same source.
Reach vs evidenceReach tells us how far a story travelled. Evidence tells us whether a mention adds independently useful facts.
ProvenanceThe record of where information first appeared and how it was copied, cited, changed or independently supported.
First observedThe earliest timestamp for a source group in the data being analysed. It is not necessarily the true moment or place where a claim originated.
Source familyA group of related publishers or domains treated as part of the same publishing organisation or network.
SyndicationWhen one publisher republishes or distributes content originally produced by another publisher.
Signal stateMediaDNA’s current label for how a mention contributes: candidate, independent evidence, first-party statement or reach-only.
Contribution scoreA prototype score estimating how much new information a source group adds. It is not a verified trust or quality rating.
MCPModel Context Protocol: a standard way for AI assistants to access tools and data from connected systems.
MFA siteA made-for-advertising website, typically designed mainly to generate advertising revenue.
