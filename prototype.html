Where the evidence layer fits beneath existing intelligence

Meltwater offers media intelligence, monitoring, reputation and AI-related product capabilities. MediaDNA is a proposed layer for persistent claim-level evidence provenance, distinguishing independent evidence from repetition. Any product connection would require separate validation and implementation.

Claim-level evidence origin

MediaDNA would record each claim’s earliest observed source and first independent evidence contribution at mention level.

Reusable evidence lineage

MediaDNA would make claim-level relationships (cites, copies, summarizes, syndicates, corroborates) available as structured provenance data.

Independent contribution

MediaDNA proposes a separate count of mentions that contribute independent new evidence, distinct from reach and repetition.

Replication role

MediaDNA would label original, derivative and mirror relationships at mention level where evidence supports the classification.

Explainable review queue

Ambiguous or high-impact items are explained and sent to a human reviewer. MediaDNA never issues a hard automated verdict.

Review decisions are local to this prototype session. Flag means “hold this item out of evidence use pending review” — it does not quarantine, delete or remove the mention. The mention remains in mention totals; a flagged independent-evidence item is excluded from the review-adjusted evidence-eligible count.

ItemDomain / scopeWhy it is in the queueOriginal signal stateSuggested useReview status / action
A reviewer confirms the signal state. A flag is a review hold, not quarantine: it remains a mention for volume reporting, but is not used as evidence while flagged. The original engine classification is preserved for auditability.
